# Network acceptance assets

The files named in `manifest.json` are downloaded directly from their official sources into
the local test-asset cache. Downloaded archives, frames, annotations, and PLY files are not
part of GS-Video and must not be committed or redistributed with the application.

DAVIS 2017 material retains its official citation: Pont-Tuset et al., *The 2017 DAVIS
Challenge on Video Object Segmentation*. Preserve that citation in research and evaluation
reports that use the material.

Graphdeco 3D Gaussian Splatting pre-trained models are restricted here to internal,
non-commercial research and evaluation. They must not be redistributed with GS-Video.
Commercial use requires explicit prior consent under the upstream license; before any
commercial release, replace these models with scenes owned by the project or licensed with
clear commercial and redistribution permission.
